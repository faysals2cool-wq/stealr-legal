Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Stealr ("Stealr", "we", "us") is an independent app based in Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the Stealr mobile app and website, why, and the choices you have. Privacy contact: in the app, Settings → Help → Contact support (our Privacy Officer under PIPEDA reads every privacy request).

Location. If you allow it, the app reads your device location once to suggest your country and search area. We do not track your location in the background. Only the city, coordinates and radius you save in a snipe are stored.

Listings. Stealr shows publicly listed marketplace items (title, price, thumbnail link, general location and posting time) and links to the original listing. We don't store sellers' names or contact details, and we don't contact sellers.

How we use information

To provide alerts and features you request, compute deal scores and profit estimates, process subscriptions, provide support, keep the service secure, and comply with law.

We use an AI service (Anthropic) to: judge whether a listing matches your search and write a one-line deal note (listing titles, prices and your search keywords); check a listing's public photos for damage when you or your plan ask for it (the listing's photos and title); write offer messages (the listing title and your offer); read an IMEI or a vehicle VIN from a photo you pick (that photo, which we don't store); name an item you photograph to look up its resale price (up to three photos, which we don't store); write a listing for something you're selling (the photos you pick, which we don't store); suggest improvements to one of your listings (its text and price); and answer questions in the in-app support chat (your messages and these account facts: plan, renewal date, number of snipes, whether notifications are on, country, sign-in methods and app version). We never send your name or email address to the AI service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Service providers

We share information only with providers who process it for us under contract: Supabase (database and authentication), Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), RevenueCat (subscription status), Anthropic (the AI features described above), Phonecheck (IMEI and serial number lookups; we send only the number you check), the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's VIN decoder (we send only the VIN you check), PostHog (product analytics without PII), Sentry (crash reports), our hosting providers, and Resend (email delivery, including your support messages and bug reports with your email address so we can reply, and the weekly “Steals you missed” email, which you can turn off at any time). Some providers are in the United States, so your information may be processed outside your province or country and be subject to the laws there.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. Listing data that isn't linked to your matches, saves or flips is deleted after about 45 days. Logs are kept for up to 90 days. Support tickets and IMEI check results are deleted with your account; copies of support emails in our support inbox are kept for [SUPPORT EMAIL RETENTION, e.g. 24 months]. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, deletion: in the app (Settings), or contact us in the app (Settings → Help → Contact support).
  • Delete your account: in the app via Settings → Delete account, or at https://stealr.app/delete-account.
  • Notifications and location: turn them off at any time in your device settings or in the app.
  • Withdraw consent (PIPEDA): you may withdraw consent to optional processing; some features may stop working.
  • US residents: depending on your state, you may have rights to know, delete, correct and opt out. We don't sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.
  • Complaints: you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local regulator.

Security

We use encryption in transit, row-level security so each user can only access their own data, and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and regulators of breaches as the law requires.

Children

Stealr is not directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post changes here and, if they are significant, tell you in the app before they take effect.
